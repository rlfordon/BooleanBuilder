Legal Boolean Search Builder

Create precise legal search strings, step-by-step.

Created by Rebecca Fordon

1. Build Your Concepts

A concept is a main idea, party, action, or legal issue. This tool will help you group synonyms for the same concept together (e.g., spectator OR fan.

2. Your Search String

Copy the final string and paste it into the search bar of your favorite legal research tool.

Note: This tool is optimized for the syntax of major legal databases like Westlaw and Lexis. Other platforms may use different rules, so always check their advanced search tips for best results.
